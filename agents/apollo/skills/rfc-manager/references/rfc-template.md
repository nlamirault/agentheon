## RFC-000: Title of the Proposal

### 1. Summary

A brief, high-level overview (one or two paragraphs) that explains the goal of this RFC without diving into deep
technical details. It should clearly state what the RFC intends to achieve.

### 2. Motivation

Explain *why* this RFC is necessary.

- What problem does it solve?
- What use cases does it support?
- What are the expected benefits or outcomes?
- Why is this change important *now*?

Provide context and link to any prior discussions, existing issues, or background information that helps a newcomer
understand the necessity of this proposal.

### 3. Guide-level Explanation

Describe the feature or change from the perspective of a user or developer who would interact with it. Write as if the
feature already exists and is being explained in documentation.

- How would someone use this feature?
- What are the primary concepts involved?
- Provide examples of usage, code snippets, or user flows.

### 4. Reference-level Explanation

Detailed technical explanation of the proposed design.

- Cover specifics, edge cases, and internal workings.
- Include architectural diagrams, pseudo-code, API changes, data structures, or other technical details as needed.
- Discuss how different components interact.

### 5. Drawbacks

Critically evaluate the potential negative impacts of the proposed solution.

- Why should we *not* do this?
- What are the costs (performance, complexity, maintenance, security, etc.)?
- What are the potential risks or downsides?

### 6. Alternatives

Discuss other approaches that were considered and why they were not chosen.

- What were the alternative solutions?
- What were their pros and cons compared to the chosen proposal?
- What is the impact of *not* implementing this change at all?

### 7. Unresolved Questions

List any open questions, areas of uncertainty, or parts of the design that are not yet fully defined.

### 8. Future Possibilities

Briefly describe potential future enhancements or related work that could build upon this RFC but are out of scope for
the current proposal.
