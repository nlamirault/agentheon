Do's & Don'ts

Do

  • Reserve gold for brand, interaction, and the active deity.
  • Set every heading in Cormorant Garamond.
  • Use mono, uppercase, wide-tracked for eyebrows & roles.
  • Create depth by stepping the background + a hairline border.
  • Keep the gold aura to one hero instance per page.

Don't

  • Use pure white #ffffff for text — use #e8e8ea.
  • Set headlines in Inter or body in Cormorant.
  • Add deity accent colors to chrome — content only.
  • Lean on drop shadows for elevation (overlay excepted).
  • Exceed 14px radius on rectangular surfaces.
